##  When Should You Use ChatGPT Plugins?

 Consider plugins as add-ons to your ChatGPT. They are the tools that empower ChatGPT to integrate with third-party services, widening its capability spectrum. Whether checking out markets, [doing math](https://win-blog.techidaily.com/1722998864296-persistently-unresponsive-heres-how-to-fix-the-latest-freezing-issues-in-new-world/), or browsing shopping deals, these plugins are your gateway to many tasks.

 According to [information about plugins on the OpenAI blog](https://openai.com/blog/chatgpt-plugins), numerous plugins help ChatGPT do a better job. Wolfram, for example, provides it with some computation and math muscle. FiscalNote offers access to datasets for legal, political, and regulatory purposes. We're sure the list will expand over time, but for now, you can check out the initial plugins on OpenAI's blog or by browsing the aforementioned plugin store.
