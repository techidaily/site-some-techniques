 The key to this deal is certification. It comes with the same warranty as a new headset, which means you don't need to worry about anything happening to your VR device.

 Here's the exact wording of the refurbished terms:

 Everything else is the same as a new headset, so it's definitely worth considering if you're in the market for one. You can use that $50 to pick up some [Quest 2 accessories](https://extra-guidance.techidaily.com/revolutionize-your-designs-incorporating-3d-text-psx-for-2024/) or games.
